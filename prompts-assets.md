# Prompts para generar assets del juego Kiari Rescue

> **Nota importante:** El juego tiene una resolución de **800x600 píxeles**. Para escenarios completos, pide fondos de **800x600 px**. Para spritesheets, pide imágenes con **fondo transparente** y dimensiones exactas.

---

## 1. Fondo completo: Casa de Laura (HouseScene)

**Prompt:**

> Create a single full background image for a 2D pixel art RPG game. The scene is the cozy living room of a young woman's house. Viewed from top-down or slightly angled top-down perspective. Dimensions exactly 800x600 pixels. Include: light brown wooden floor, cream-colored walls, a front door at the top center, a pink dog bed on the right side, a brown sofa with cushions on the left, a small coffee table, a potted plant, a TV stand, warm daylight coming from a window. Cute, colorful, retro 16-bit pixel art style. No characters. No text. No transparent areas.

**Archivo de salida sugerido:** `bg-casa.png`  
**Tamaño exacto:** 800×600 píxeles

---

## 6. Laura (protagonista)

**Prompt:**

> Create a pixel art character spritesheet for a top-down 2D RPG game. The character is a young woman named Laura, with medium brown skin, brown hair tied in a messy bun or ponytail, wearing a pink t-shirt, blue jeans, and white sneakers. She should look cute, brave and slightly nerdy. The spritesheet must be in a 4x3 grid: 4 rows for directions (down, left, right, up) and 3 columns for animation frames (idle, left foot, right foot). Each frame is 32x32 pixels. Use a limited 16-bit color palette, clean pixel art with black outlines, transparent background. Style: retro GameBoy Advance / SNES RPG.

**Archivo de salida sugerido:** `laura.png`  
**Tamaño:** 96×128 píxeles (3 cols × 4 rows de 32×32)

---

## 7. Kiari (la perrita)

She has scruffy tan and gray fur, small pointed ears, a short muzzle, and a fluffy tail. Very cute and expressive. The spritesheet must be in a 4x3 grid: 4 rows for directions (down, left, right, up) and 3 columns for animation frames (idle, left foot, right foot). Each frame is 24x24 pixels. Use limited 16-bit color palette, clean pixel art with soft black outlines, transparent background. Style: retro GameBoy Advance RPG.

**Archivo de salida sugerido:** `kiari.png`  
**Tamaño:** 72×96 píxeles (3 cols × 4 rows de 24×24)

## 10. Spritesheet organizado con todos los elementos del juego (Opción A)

Si prefieres que un solo artista haga todos los objetos pequeños en una sola imagen organizada, usa este prompt:

**Prompt:**

> Create a pixel art spritesheet containing all items for a cute 2D game called "Kiari Rescue". Transparent background. Organized in a grid with each item separated by empty space and clearly distinct. Include these items exactly:
> - A small yellow envelope clue with a red exclamation mark (32x32)
> - A pink dog bed (64x48)
> - A brown sofa from top-down (96x64)
> - A potted plant (32x48)
> - A wooden door from top-down (48x48)
> - A small pizzeria counter with pizza boxes (96x64)
> - A white pillow with pink edges for a pillow fight (64x40)
> - A fluffy heart (32x32)
> - A disco ball (48x48)
> Clean pixel art, limited 16-bit palette, black outlines. Style: retro GameBoy Advance / SNES RPG.

**Archivo:** `items-game.png`  
**Tamaño sugerido:** 512×512 píxeles
